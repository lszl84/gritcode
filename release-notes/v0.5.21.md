## What's new

**🧹 No more overlapping text in the chat.** Opening and closing side panels, or resizing and re-tiling the window, could leave chat messages drawn on top of each other: a bubble spilling into the paragraph below, table rows over the next line. We tracked it down to stale height measurements in the layout cache and fixed it at the root, with no cost to performance. The chat now stays clean however much you rearrange your workspace.

**🤖 Layout self-check.** The local control interface gains `checkLayout` and `scrollToBlock`, so the chat's layout can be verified automatically and this bug stays fixed.
