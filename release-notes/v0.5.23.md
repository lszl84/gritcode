## What's new

**🙅 gritcode stays out of your way.** When the agent finished a turn while you were working in another window, gritcode would jump to the front, and on Hyprland even yank you to its workspace. Not anymore: let the agent work in the background and carry on with what you're doing. If you're already in gritcode when a turn ends, the cursor still lands right back in the input, ready for your next message.
